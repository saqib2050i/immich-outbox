Testing tools
These only affect this service. Nothing here deletes anything from Immich, and nothing can remove photos already uploaded to Google Photos — clear those yourself at photos.google.com. Files removed from the outbox are deleted on the Pixel too, once Syncthing catches up.

Retry the failed ones

Clears the attempt counter on anything that gave up after five tries. Use it after fixing a permission or connection problem.

Keeps: everything. Nothing is deleted.

Rescan Immich now

Walks the whole library immediately instead of waiting for the next scheduled scan. Use it after adding photos to Immich.

Keeps: everything. Nothing is deleted.

Empty the outbox

Deletes everything currently waiting, and puts those photos back in the queue to be sent again. Use it when the outbox holds something wrong.

Keeps: what has already been backed up, and your settings.

Send everything again

Forgets what has been sent and starts the whole library over. The usual one while testing.

Keeps: your settings, and which files are motion-photo clips.

Start fresh

Clears the ledger and activity log completely, empties the outbox, and rescans Immich from scratch.

Keeps: your settings, including the API key.

Ledger backups

The ledger is the only record of what has already been sent. Lose it and everything is re-sent, which means a duplicate of your whole library in Google Photos. Backed up daily, last 14 kept.

For a clean test, also delete the contents of DCIM/ImmichQueue on the Pixel and remove the test uploads from Google Photos. Otherwise Syncthing pushes the old files back, and re-sending gives you duplicates.
