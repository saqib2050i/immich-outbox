Right now

Photos and videos move in separate lanes, several at once, filling the outbox until it is full — then they wait, because the outbox only empties when the phone has uploaded them to Google Photos. What is waiting to go, and which months it belongs to, lives in Library.

In the outbox

Already copied out and mirrored to the phone by Syncthing. These are out of our hands: they disappear on their own once Google Photos has them, and that disappearing is the only proof of backup there is. Cancelling pulls one back — "send later" returns it to the queue, "don't send" takes it out of the running.

    Dates corrected in Immich

    These were re-dated in Immich after they were imported, so the file still carries the old date. Google Photos keeps whatever date it is first given, so they are held back until you decide. Sending them writes the corrected date into each file as it goes out — the only case where an original is altered, and only its date.

      Testing tools
      These only affect this service. Nothing here deletes anything from Immich, and nothing can remove photos already uploaded to Google Photos — clear those yourself at photos.google.com. Files removed from the outbox are deleted on the Pixel too, once Syncthing catches up.

      Undo every exclusion

      Puts everything marked "not being sent" back in the running, across every month. Months and single files can be restored individually in Library; this is for undoing a sweep.

      Restored

      Retry the failed ones

      Clears the attempt counter on anything that gave up after five tries. Use it after fixing a permission or connection problem.

      Keeps: everything. Nothing is deleted.

      Rescan Immich now

      Walks the whole library immediately instead of waiting for the next scheduled scan. Use it after adding photos to Immich.

      Keeps: everything. Nothing is deleted.

      Empty the outbox

      Deletes everything currently waiting, and puts those photos back in the queue to be sent again. Use it when the outbox holds something wrong.

      Keeps: what has already been backed up, and your settings.

      Send everything again

      Forgets what has been sent and starts the whole library over. The usual one while testing.

      Keeps: your settings, and which files are motion-photo clips.

      Start fresh

      Clears the ledger and activity log completely, empties the outbox, and rescans Immich from scratch.

      Keeps: your settings, including the API key.

      Trace one file

      Follows a single file from Immich into the outbox and reports what is in all three copies, so a file that arrived damaged and a file this service damaged stop looking the same. Nothing is written; Immich's copy is fetched, read and thrown away.

      The name exactly as Immich holds it. A file that has never been sent has no second copy to compare, which is what the second button is for.

      Ledger backups

      The ledger is the only record of what has already been sent. Lose it and everything is re-sent, which means a duplicate of your whole library in Google Photos. Backed up daily, last 14 kept.

      For a clean test, also delete the contents of DCIM/ImmichQueue on the Pixel and remove the test uploads from Google Photos. Otherwise Syncthing pushes the old files back, and re-sending gives you duplicates.
